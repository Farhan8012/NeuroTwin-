# Privacy and Ethics

> [!warning] Pre-Deployment Review Requirement
> This document outlines critical privacy, security, and ethical protocols. This note **must be formally revisited and updated** prior to any real-world deployment or clinical user testing beyond non-production prototyping.

---

## 1. Biometric Sensitivity & Vulnerable Population Protection

Face embeddings generated by InsightFace are mathematical representations of human facial geometry. They constitute **sensitive biometric data**.

- **Vulnerable End-Users:** Dementia and memory-impaired patients may have compromised capacity to understand or grant formal digital consent regarding continuous visual scanning.
- **Protection Principles:** Biometric datasets stored in Qdrant must be strictly partitioned per household. Cross-patient dataset pooling or public cloud indexing is strictly prohibited.

---

## 2. Consent Framework & Surrogate Administration

Because caregivers register family members and manage data on behalf of the patient:
- **Caregiver Authorization:** Caregivers act as legal proxies, but explicit consent flows must exist for third parties (e.g., visiting friends, extended family, home health aides) whose photos and information are uploaded to the system.
- **Right to be Forgotten:** The caregiver interface must provide simple one-click mechanisms to purge specific person profiles, facial embeddings, and associated memory payloads from the Qdrant database.

---

## 3. Data Minimization Principles

NeuroTwin bakes data minimization directly into its core technical architecture:
- **Zero Raw Video Storage:** Video frames are captured strictly in volatile RAM memory on the Android device. Raw video feeds are **never saved to disk** or recorded.
- **On-Device Gating:** Frames evaluated by ML Kit that contain no detected faces or relevant objects are dropped immediately in memory, preventing unnecessary data transmission.
- **Ephemeral Audio Buffering:** Voice query audio files sent to Whisper are deleted from server storage immediately after transcription and response generation.

---

## 4. Data Retention, Encryption & Access Control

Key questions to govern technical implementation:
- **Encryption at Rest:** Is Qdrant payload data encrypted at rest on the M4 MacBook Air storage drive? (AES-256 volume encryption or filesystem-level FileVault encryption required).
- **Access Control & Authentication:** Caregiver REST APIs must require authenticated token access (e.g., OAuth2 / JWT with HTTPS/TLS) to prevent unauthorized local network access to stored memories, medical records, or family photos.
- **Local Network Isolation:** Operating over a local Wi-Fi network or an encrypted WireGuard VPN tunnel minimizes exposure to public internet interceptors.

---

## 5. Scope Disclaimer & Regulatory Boundaries

> [!warning] Medical Device Disclaimer
> NeuroTwin is designed purely as an assistive memory-support and cognitive companion tool. It is **NOT** a diagnostic, therapeutic, or medical device, and has not been evaluated by the FDA or medical regulatory bodies.
> 
> It must never replace professional medical supervision, emergency response systems, or certified caregiving personnel.
